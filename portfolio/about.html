Corrao Elena

Biography

Elena Corrao is a model who grew up in a small town, one of three girls and a rebel without a cause. She modeled for a couple of years, then moved to Florida to build her skills as an entrepreneur.

She is led by curiosity. Growth, new places, food, and cultures she hasn’t met yet all pull at her, and she cares about sustainability and diversity in the work she takes on. She has never been afraid to be a little weird, and that willingness to stand out is what she brings to every frame.

Now that she has the skills to do both, she is ready to take on the world. She is out looking for people to collaborate with, make art, and keep discovering.
