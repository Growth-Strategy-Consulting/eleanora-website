Door Seven · Libra

The Company You Keep

People
Read

You become the room you stand in.

Slowly, quietly, without ever agreeing to it.

You've had both kinds of people. The one you leave feeling lighter, taller, like your life is actually possible. And the one you leave a little smaller, a little more tired, a little more talked out of yourself. Same you. Two different rooms. And you walked out of each one carrying exactly what it handed you.

Here's what nobody says out loud. You don't rise or fall on your own. You drift toward the middle of whoever you're around. Their standards become your standards. Their fears become the walls of your world. Their ceiling quietly becomes yours. You will not out-dream a room that thinks small. You'll just get very good at explaining why you never went for it.

You know the drain. The one who goes quiet when you win and lights up when you struggle. Who calls your dream cute. Who needs you a little stuck, because your staying small is what keeps them comfortable. So I'll say the hard thing. Loving someone is not a good enough reason to let them cost you your whole becoming. You can love people from further away.

And you know the builder too. The one who talks about you like you've already done the thing. Who's genuinely, uncomplicatedly happy when you win. Who tells you the truth you don't want to hear, because they'd rather you be great than comfortable. That last one is the rarest, and it's the whole game.

The people who love you most are the ones brave enough to tell you the truth. Be that for someone. Keep the ones who are that for you.

So here's Libra's whole assignment, and it cuts both ways. Choose your room on purpose. Get close to the people already living some version of what you want, and let the ones who shrink you drift a little further away. Guilt and all.

Then be worth keeping. Be the friend people want in their corner for the long haul. Tell the truth, kindly. Clap the loudest when someone wins. Become the room you'd want to walk into.

Because in the end you don't drift toward what you want. You drift toward who you're with. Look around. That's where you're headed. If you like it, stay.

If you don't, you already know what the next brave move is.

xo, Eleanora
